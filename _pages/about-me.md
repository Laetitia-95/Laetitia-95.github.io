I recently earned my **Master of Science in Supply Chain Analytics** from CSUSM. My background has taken me through different fields before arriving in supply chain analytics, from an academic background in healthcare to experience in business and operations.  

## My Background

Before getting into business and analytics, I earned a **Bachelor's degree in Occupational Therapy** in France. In 2021, I moved to the United States and spent two years working as an au pair, an experience that allowed to adapt to a new environment and explore different opportunities. 

Later, I pursued an **Associate degree in Business** and gained experience working for a small business. In this role, I communicated with suppliers, ordered products and supplies for a new store, helped set up new products in QuickBooks, organized inventory, and fulfilled online orders. I also helped prepare and organize holiday gift baskets that contributed to the company's revenue. 

These experiences increased my interest in how businesses manage products, inventory and operations, which led me to pursue the master in Supply Chain Analytics. During this program, I developed technical skills in data analysis, visualization, forecasting and predictive analytics and applied them to supply chain an operational problems. 

## What I'm Looking For

I am looking to begin my career in a role where I can apply my analytical skills to **supply chain, operations, or business problems**. I am particularly interested in opportunities involving data analysis, and inventory and supply chain planning. 

I also have a strong interest in **project management** and enjoy work that involves coordinating activities, solving problems, and helping move projects from planning to execution. I am especially interested in roles where **project management and analytics overlap**. 

As I begin my career, I am looking for an opportunity where I can continue developing my technical skills, gain hands-on industry expereince, and contribute to data-driven operational decisions.
