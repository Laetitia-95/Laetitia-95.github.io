During my **Master of Science in Supply Chain Analytics**, I completed coursework combining data analytics, supply chain management, operations, and business decision-making. 

The courses below highlight the main areas of study and the analytical and technical skills I developed in the program. 

## Analytics & Data Visualization 
### OM 620 - Tools and Technologies for Analytics 
This course introduced the foundations of data analytics and the technical tools used, including **Python, SQL, VS Code, and GitHub**. It covered data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis with **Python**, as well as working with **SQL and SQLite** for data quarying and transformation. 

A course project used **Python to safety stock analysis**, comparing inventory requirements across different service levels and examining the relationship between service targets and safety stock. 

**Related project**: [Safety Stock Analysis - Python](/portfolio/safety-stock-analysis-python/)

### OM 621 - Advanced Visual Analytics
This course explored the principles of **data storytelling and effective visualization**. It also covered reproducible information visualization using the **grammar of graphics**, advanced visualizations, geographic mapping, and visualization refinement in **Python**. 

The course also focused on developing interactive business intelligence skills using **Power BI**, including **Power Query, DAX, forecasting with ETS, and interactive dashboards** to transform and communicate analytical results.

**Related projects**: [Transportation Cost Analysis - Python](/portfolio/transportation-cost-analysis-python/) and [Transportation Cost Analysis - Power BI](/portfolio/transportation-cost-analysis-powerbi/)

### OM 623 - Predictive Analytics Applications
This course developed and evaluated predictive models using **R**, including multiple linear regression, logistic regression, regression and classification trees, and artificial neural networks. I learned about feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, model evaluation, and interpretation techniques to real-world datasets. 

**Related project**: [Consumer Electricity Rate Prediction - R](/portfolio/electricity-rate-prediction-r/)

### OM 640 - Supply Chain Analytics
This course applied quantitative methods to supply chain planning and decision-making, including demand forecasting, invnetory management, and service-level decisions. Decision models were developed in **Excel** using tools such as **Goal Seek and Solver** to evaluate supply chain alternatives. 

Forecasting methods included naïve and moving-average models, regression-based forecasting, and exponential smoothing methods such as **Holts and Winters' models**, with model performance evaluated using MAPE, RMSE for example. **ForecastX** was also used for forecasting analysis. 

**Related project**: [Supply Chain Decision Models - Excel](/portfolio/supply-chain-decision-models-excel)

## Supply Chain & Operations
### OM 631 - Sustainable Operations & SCM
This course developed a broad understanding of **end-to-end supply chain management and operations**, including supply chain strategy, sourcing, procurement, inventory, logistics, transportation, sustainability, and coordination across the supply chain among others. A final project applied these concepts to an analysis of **Ferrari's supply chain**, including its supply strategies (sourcing, procurement, manufacturing, distribution and logistics and sustainability), Information System & Technology (ITS), risk management, and performance measurement. 

### OM 633 - ERP and Supply Chain Technology
This course explored how business information systems and **enterprise resource planning (ERP)** support supply chain planning and operations. The topics covered included sales and operations planning, aggregate planning, **master production scheduling (MPS), material requirements planning (MRP), manufacturing resource planning (MRP II)**,a dn push/pull, just-in-time (JIT), and lean systems. The coursework included resolving problems using MPS, MRP and MRP II,a dn guided cases using **SAP and NetSuite**, providing an experience to ERP systems. 

### OM 635 - Strategic Total Cost Management
This course applied the total cost management method to purchasing and supply chain decisions, including **price analysis, price discipline model, process-based cost model, should-cost model, and total cost of ownership**. It encouraged to look beyond the purchase price to understand the underlying drivers of cost and evaluate sourcing decisions from a broader total-cost perspective. 

Related project: [Should-Cost Analysis](/portfolio/should-cost-analysis-excel)

### OM 639 - Facility Logistics
This course explored the design, operation, and assessment of **warehouses and distribution facilities**. The topics included warehouse functions and strategy, warehouse types and material flow, storage policies, Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), automation, benchmarking and order-picking systems and strategies. It also covered warehouse data profiling and facility assessment with the use of operational KPIs and employee, customer and shareholder-facing measures to evaluate warehouse performance. 

Related project: [Warehouse Assessment](/portfolio/warehouse-assessment)
