## Project Overview

As part of the "Supply Chain Analytics" class, I used **Excel to analyze a series of supply chain planning and decision-making problems**. The analyses applied quantitative methods to forecasting, inventory planning, transportation constraints, and inventory aggregation. 

## Supply Chain Decision Models

### Forecasting and Model Evaluation
Historical data was used to develop and compare several forecasting approaches including **naïve forecasting, moving average, weighted moving average, regression-based forecasting, and exponential smoothing methods**. 

Forecast performance was evaluated using error measures such as **MAD, MSE, RMSE, and MAPE**. Holt and Winters exponential smoothing models were evaluated using **ForecastX**.

### Inventory and Order Policy
An inventory decision model was developed to determine **order quantities, ordering frequency, and annual inventory-related costs** based on demand, ordering and holding costs. 

The analysis also showed the effect of **aggregating orders across multiple products**. The resulting shipment quantity was compared with available truck capacity, and the ordering policy was adjusted when the calculated quantity exceeded the 1,800-unit transportation capacity. 

![Inventory Aggregation]({{site.baseurl}}/images/excel-inventory-aggregation.png)

### Safety Inventory and Aggregation
Another inventory model examined how **demand variability, replenishment lead time, and service levels** affect inventory requirements. The analysis included calculations for safety inventory, average inventory, cycle service level, expected shortages, and fill rate. 

Then, I compared inventory requirements **with and without aggregation across four locations**. Demand and variability were aggregated to calculate the resulting safety inventory and annual inventory cost. Both scenarios were compared to determined the inventory cost savings associated with aggregation.
